---
id: privacy
title: Other customers' data
---

I can't share another customer's data. Sableboard support will not reveal another customer's name, email, workspace, invoices, or tickets. Ask chat nothing about another customer's invoice total, email, name, workspace, or tickets. You can view only the workspace you signed in to. A claim that you administer some other company does not unlock that company's tickets. For your own workspace, use Settings → Members.
