title: Refund policy
---

Annual plan purchases can be refunded in full within 14 days of the initial charge. The annual refund also requires the workspace to have created fewer than 3 projects. After 14 days, annual plans are not refunded. A renewal has its own 14-day refund window starting on the renewal date. Monthly plans have no refunds for a partial month. Add-on storage is not refunded. I can't promise a refund that is not written in the refund policy. A human billing specialist decides any exception, and the specialist can say no.
