id: failed-payments
title: Failed payments
---

If a renewal charge fails, Sableboard emails the workspace admin the same day. The workspace stays on its paid plan for 10 days after the failed payment. After those 10 days the workspace is downgraded to Sprout. Updating the card under Settings → Billing retries the charge. Sableboard does not charge a late fee during those 10 days.
