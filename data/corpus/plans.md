id: plans
title: Plans and prices
---

Sableboard has three plans. The Sprout plan is free and includes 3 members and 2 projects. The Grove plan costs $12 per member per month when billed monthly. The Grove plan costs $120 per member per year when billed yearly. The Canopy plan costs $28 per member per month when billed monthly. The Canopy plan costs $280 per member per year when billed yearly. Canopy includes SSO and an audit log. Sprout does not include SSO.
